Shop everyday groceries from Koya Stores and choose convenient store pickup or local delivery.

Browse products by category, search the catalogue, manage your cart and select an available fulfilment slot. Eligible offers are applied transparently, including order discounts and free-product promotions configured by the store.

With Koya Stores you can:

• Browse the current grocery catalogue
• Search products and check prices
• Choose pickup or local delivery where available
• Save and manage delivery addresses
• Select an available date and time slot
• Follow your order status
• Receive order updates when notifications are enabled
• View order details and previous orders
• Manage your profile, privacy choices and account deletion

Availability, service areas, prices, stock, delivery charges, slots and promotions are controlled by Koya Stores and may change. Internet access and a verified customer account are required for live ordering.
