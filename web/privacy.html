Privacy Policy

Effective 6 October 2026

Koya Stores uses personal information only to provide customer accounts, prepare pickup and local-delivery orders, support customers, protect the service and show order updates.

Information we collect

  • Account identifiers, including email address and internal user ID.
  • Profile details such as name and phone number.
  • Saved delivery addresses, recipient details and delivery instructions. If you choose to share a delivery pin, its coordinates, estimated accuracy and capture time are saved with your address and delivery orders. Location is requested only while you use this feature; we do not track your movements.
  • Cart, order, fulfilment, discount and payment-status information.
  • Limited technical records needed to prevent abuse and diagnose service failures.

How we use information

We use this information to authenticate customers, show the catalogue, validate stock and prices, fulfil orders, arrange delivery, provide support, show order status and secure the service. We do not sell personal information or use it for third-party advertising.

Service providers

Koya Stores uses Supabase for authentication, database, storage and server functions. Supabase processes information on Koya Stores' instructions under its own security and privacy terms. The first app release does not include third-party advertising or native online-payment SDKs. Device notifications are optional. When background notifications are configured and enabled, Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service process a device identifier and order-update payload to deliver alerts. Messaging SDKs also declare limited diagnostics and messaging-service analytics; we do not use these for advertising or track people for advertising. Opening a delivery pin in Maps shares the selected coordinates with your chosen map provider.

Retention and deletion

A cart containing product identifiers and quantities is saved in encrypted storage on your device, separately for each account. Signing out keeps it for your next sign-in on that device. Confirmed purchases remove the purchased quantities. Account deletion removes that account's saved cart. Clearing device or browser storage may also remove it.

Account, profile and saved-address data is retained while the account is active. A customer can permanently delete the account from Profile → Privacy and account → Delete account. Completed transaction records may be retained where required for accounting, fraud prevention or legal obligations, but customer names, phone numbers, addresses, delivery pins and instructions are removed from those retained records during account deletion.

An active pickup or delivery must be completed or cancelled before self-service deletion so the store does not lose details required to fulfil it. Customers who cannot access the app can use the public account deletion request page.

Security and choices

Data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections. Access controls restrict customer records to the customer and authorized staff. Customers can edit profile and address information, remove saved addresses and delete their account from the app.

Children

The service is intended for adults arranging household purchases and is not directed to children under 13.
