Delete your account

You can permanently delete your Koya Stores customer account and its associated personal data. Deletion cannot be undone.

Delete from the app

  1. Sign in to the Koya Stores app.
  2. Open Profile.
  3. Select Privacy and account, then Delete account.
  4. Read the notice, type DELETE and confirm.
Active pickup or delivery orders must first be completed or cancelled so the store retains the contact information needed to fulfil the order.

Request deletion without app access

Call Koya Stores and ask for a customer account deletion request. Have the email address used for the account available. Staff will require secure account-ownership verification before processing the request.

What is deleted

  • The sign-in account and email identifier.
  • Profile name and phone number.
  • Saved addresses, recipient details and delivery instructions.
  • Notification device tokens and personal offer-redemption records.

Completed transaction records may be retained where necessary for accounting, fraud prevention or legal obligations. They are disconnected from the account and customer names, phone numbers, addresses and delivery instructions are removed.

See the Koya Stores Privacy Policy for details.
