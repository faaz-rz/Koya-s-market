# Product image attribution

## Retailer and manufacturer web sources

The `web/` directory contains 1,351 optimized product-family images matched to
2,729 active catalogue rows using the corrected storefront brand, product
name, product form, and available pack size. Each product's original page URL,
image URL, source host, match method, and available confidence data are recorded
in `.codex_work/product_master/product_image_matches.json`.

These source records provide traceability; they do not transfer ownership of
the original photography. Before public production distribution, the store
owner should replace or approve these images using manufacturer, distributor,
or billing-catalogue assets for which they have suitable usage rights.

## Open Food Facts

Open Food Facts, Open Beauty Facts, and Open Products Facts currently provide
40 distinct image assets used by 43 active catalogue rows: 35 Open Food Facts
rows, seven Open Beauty Facts rows, and one Open Products Facts row. The newer
matches use exact product barcodes and the cross-database `product_type=all`
lookup. Their individual source pages and image URLs are recorded in the image
manifest.
